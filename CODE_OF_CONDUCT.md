# Code of Conduct

ADHD Healing should stay useful, respectful, and technically focused for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in code review, issues, discussions, and documentation updates.
- Keep criticism specific to code, behavior, data, docs, or process.
- Assume good intent, but be direct about concrete problems.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and language preferences. English and Chinese are both acceptable.
- Prefer transparent, public discussion for decisions that affect other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior based on identity, background, or personal traits.
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual imagery unrelated to the project.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith derailment.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository spaces related to ADHD Healing, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and review threads.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations. They may also warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and keep collaboration workable.

## Reporting

If a conduct issue should not be reported in public, contact the repository owner through GitHub. Reports should include links, screenshots, or other concrete context when possible.

## Project Standard

This document is intentionally lightweight. It can be updated as the contributor community grows or the project workflow changes.
